## Three ways to play it

**A — Re-quote the delta (recommended if he values it).**
Bill an additional Phase-2 fee. Conservative ask: **$1,500–$2,500.** That brings the project to
~$3,000–$4,000 total — still very reasonable for a full custom e-commerce backend with fulfillment,
dashboards, sales, and preview/publish. (For reference, off-the-shelf this is Shopify at $29–79/mo
*forever* plus app fees; you built him the owned version.)

**B — Hold at $1,400 as goodwill, add a retainer.**
Keep the build price as a relationship/portfolio play, but set up a **monthly retainer** for hosting +
small updates + support: **$75–150/mo.** Recurring revenue beats a one-time bump for the land goal, and
he already leans on you for "whatever fits in an email" changes.

**C — Hybrid (the usual sweet spot).**
Modest one-time bump to acknowledge the jump in value (**+$500–$750**) *and* a small retainer
(**$75–150/mo**). Gets you paid fairly now and sets up recurring.

## Honest read

You underprice (we've flagged this before). Even Option A's low end is fair — this is a $3k–5k+ build

you gift some of it — that protects the relationship and your next quote.
