Privacy Policy.

Last updated: May 26, 2026

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. We respect your privacy and only collect what we need to deliver your order.

What we collect

  • Order information: name, email, phone, shipping address, items purchased, payment confirmation from Stripe.
  • Uploaded supply lists: photos or PDFs you upload. We process these to build your kit and delete them within 30 days of order completion.
  • Site usage: standard web logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and analytics.

How we use it

  • To process and ship your order.
  • To send order confirmation and shipping update emails.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • To improve the site and detect fraud.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers we use

  • Stripe processes payments. They store your payment information per their privacy policy.
  • Resend delivers transactional emails.
  • Anthropic (Claude) parses uploaded supply lists. Images are sent for processing and not retained by Anthropic for training.
  • Render hosts our website and database.

Children's privacy

Our site is intended for parents and guardians, not children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies, only what is needed for cart functionality and security. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. Email the contact below.
