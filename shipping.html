Shipping Policy.

Last updated: May 26, 2026

We ship every order from our warehouse via USPS or UPS Ground.

Rates

Orders $35 and up Free shipping
Orders under $35 $5.00 flat

Delivery time

  • Orders ship within 1 business day of payment.
  • Standard transit time: 3 to 7 business days within the continental US.
  • You'll receive an email with tracking information when your kit ships.

Back-to-school cutoff

To guarantee delivery before the first day of school, order at least 10 business days in advance. Orders placed after the cutoff will ship in the order received and may arrive after the first day.

Where we ship

We currently ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship internationally.

Lost or damaged packages

If your package arrives damaged or doesn't arrive within 14 business days of the ship date, contact us and we'll replace it at no charge.
