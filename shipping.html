Shipping Policy.

Last updated: June 6, 2026

We ship every order from our warehouse via USPS or UPS Ground.

Rates

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on the size of your order. You'll always see the exact amount before you pay.

Tracking

You'll receive an email with tracking information as soon as your order ships, so you can follow it right to your door.

Where we ship

We currently ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship internationally.

Lost or damaged packages

If your package arrives damaged or doesn't arrive, contact us and we'll work to resolve it. For damaged packages, please include photos of both the damaged box and the damaged product when you contact us. Replacements are subject to review.

Contact

For shipping questions, email us at Main@fasttrackschoolsupplies.com.
