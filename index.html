Welcome to Fast Track School Supplies.

Fast Track School Supplies helps parents get back to school ready without the last minute stress.

We created this service for families who want school supplies done right without running from store to store or guessing what to buy. Our goal is simple. Save parents time and make sure schools get the supplies students actually need.

How it works.

Four straightforward steps from supply list to front door.

Upload your supply list

Snap a photo, upload a PDF, or paste the text. We accept any format your school sends home.

We match what we can

Our team confirms each product against your list. Anything we can't match, we flag for you to review.

Order online

Review your kit, confirm details, and check out securely. One cart for every kid in the house.

Delivered to your door

Every item labeled and packed. Local delivery and nationwide shipping available.
